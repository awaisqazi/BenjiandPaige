Overnight
Accommodations

We have arranged for exclusive use of the entire camp for the duration of our wedding (Sunday the 4th into Monday the 5th) and would love nothing more than for you to join us from beginning to end.

While we know a wedding overnight might be unusual, we hope you'll reserve your cabin and stay over. We've planned an exciting schedule filled with family, friends, adventure, dancing & s'mores. You won't want to miss any of it!

🛏️

Cabins include between 2 and 5 beds with private bathrooms, A/C & heat. Bedding, room towels, and housekeeping are included.

📋

Club Getaway will collect reservations first, then we'll help assign cabins. Individuals and couples get private rooms, and families will be kept together whenever possible. Have a specific request? Note it when you book or in the RSVP comments!
